    Questions

    What clients ask before they start.

    Do I need GoHighLevel already?

    No. We can set up a new account for you, move your contacts and pipelines over from another CRM, or work inside the account you already have.

    Will the AI agents sound robotic?

    We train every agent on your services, prices and tone of voice, and test it on real calls and messages before it goes live. Anything it can't handle is passed to your team.

    Can you work under my agency's brand?

    Yes. White-label clients get everything delivered under their own logo and domain. We never contact your clients unless you ask us to.

    What is Knock Knock AI, and what do you do for it?

    Knock Knock AI is a white-label AI sales platform for agencies. It adds chat, call and live video agents to a website and scores each visitor's buying intent. We're an official Knock Knock AI white-label support partner: we set it up under your brand, onboard your clients, connect it to your CRM and support it day to day.

    Do you manage the ad spend too?

    We manage the campaigns. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account, so you always keep control of the budget and the data.

    How soon will I see results?

    AI agents and follow-up automations work from the day they go live. Ad campaigns usually need two to four weeks of data to settle.

    Is my customer data safe?

    Your data stays in your own accounts. We only use the access each automation needs, and we're happy to sign an NDA before we start.

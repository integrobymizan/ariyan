    What we automate

    Six workflows that give teams their week back.

    Most of our work falls into these areas. Each one runs inside your existing tools, with a person kept in the loop wherever judgement matters.

    Lead capture and follow-up

    Every enquiry from your site, inbox or ads is qualified, logged in your CRM and answered within minutes, day or night.

    Replaces manual CRM entry, slow first replies

    Customer support agents

    An AI agent trained on your help docs answers routine tickets and hands the tricky ones to a person with the full context attached.

    Replaces copy-paste answers, ticket triage

    Finance and back office

    Invoices raised, matched and chased. Receipts read, coded and filed in Xero or QuickBooks before month end arrives.

    Replaces reconciliation, payment chasing

    Document processing

    Contracts, forms and PDFs read by AI, with the key fields pulled out and pushed into the systems that need them.

    Replaces retyping data from PDFs

    Internal knowledge assistant

    Ask a question in Slack or Teams and get an answer drawn from your own policies, SOPs and past projects, with sources linked.

    Replaces hunting through shared drives

    Reporting and ops alerts

    Weekly numbers compiled and sent automatically, with an alert the moment something moves outside its normal range.

    Replaces Monday-morning spreadsheets

    How an engagement runs

    From audit to live workflow in about five weeks.

    1. 01 Week 1

      Audit

      We map how work actually moves through your team and rank every task by hours saved against effort to automate.

      Output: automation roadmap

    2. 02 Week 2

      Blueprint

      You sign off a written spec for the first workflow: triggers, tools, edge cases and where a person stays in the loop.

      Output: workflow spec

    3. 03 Weeks 3–5

      Build and test

      We build inside your accounts, test against real past data and run it alongside your team before switching it on.

      Output: live workflow

    4. 04 Ongoing

      Run and improve

      We monitor every run, fix failures fast and send a monthly report on hours saved and what to automate next.

      Output: monthly report

    Questions

    What teams ask before they start.

    Is our data safe?

    We build inside your own accounts wherever we can, use AI providers that do not train on your data, and sign an NDA and data processing agreement before we see anything. Access is limited to what each workflow needs.

    Do we have to change the tools we use?

    No. We connect the software you already pay for. If a tool has no API we will tell you during the audit and suggest the simplest workaround.

    What happens when an automation fails?

    Every workflow retries on its own, logs what happened and alerts us. Anything it cannot handle is passed to a named person on your team with the context they need.

    Will this replace my staff?

    It replaces tasks, not people. Our clients use the hours they get back for sales, service and the work they hired those people to do.

    How soon will something be live?

    A pilot workflow is usually running in production about four weeks after the audit call.

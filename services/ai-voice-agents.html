Questions

AI voice agents, answered.

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

The agent can introduce itself as an AI assistant, and we recommend that it does. Most callers care more about a quick, clear answer than about who gives it.

Can it transfer to a real person?

Yes. You decide when it hands over, for example for complaints, urgent jobs or VIP clients, and which number it transfers to.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. We can forward your existing number, or set up a new local number if you prefer.

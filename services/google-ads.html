Questions

How much should I spend?

It depends on your area and competition. We'll recommend a starting budget on the strategy call, and you can change it at any time.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Ad spend is paid directly to Google from your own account, so you keep the data and the history.

How soon will I see leads?

Search campaigns can bring enquiries in the first week. Results usually improve over the first one to three months as data builds.

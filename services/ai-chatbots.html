Questions

AI chatbots, answered.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

We set guardrails so it only answers from information you approve, and it hands over to your team when it isn't sure. We review conversations weekly and correct anything that's off.

Which channels can it work on?

Your website chat widget, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger and SMS. Google Business Profile chat can be added where it's available.

Can it take bookings?

Yes. It connects to your calendar, offers available times and books the appointment inside the chat.
